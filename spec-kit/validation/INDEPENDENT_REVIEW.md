# Independent Review Protocol
Version: 1.0.0

Reviewer must not rely solely on the orchestrator's summary or agent conclusions.

## Required review
1. Re-read exact product identity evidence.
2. Verify size chart scope and exact 110 / equivalent mapping.
3. Recalculate total payable price and discount from source values.
4. Check whether coupons/card offers are conditional.
5. Confirm fiber composition and distinguish shell/lining/fill.
6. Inspect stock at exact size/color option and timestamp.
7. Check seller identity, return policy, and listing condition.
8. Search for counterevidence, model-season mismatch, and contradictory reviews.
9. Re-run hard gates from raw structured records.
10. Record defects, severity, evidence references, and pass/fail.

## Reviewer outcomes
- PASS: no unresolved critical/high-severity defects.
- PASS_WITH_WARNINGS: only non-critical limitations; clearly disclosed.
- FAIL: any hard constraint or decision-critical evidence defect.
- NOT_REVIEWED: reviewer evidence unavailable.

BUY is forbidden unless reviewer outcome is PASS or explicitly permitted PASS_WITH_WARNINGS and all hard gates pass.
